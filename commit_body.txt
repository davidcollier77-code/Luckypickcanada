Fix: Assign AudioContext.currentTime to webAudioOriginRef.current

The Standard lucky card reveal audio was missing because the Web Audio timing origin
(`webAudioOriginRef.current`) was never initialized from the active `AudioContext.currentTime`.
This caused scheduled playback events (`source.start(...)`) to use incorrect timestamps or fail outright.

This commit restores the Standard audio implementation by assigning `webAudioOriginRef.current = audioCtxRef.current.currentTime;`
at the beginning of the `scheduleWebAudio` block inside `app/lucky-card-reveal.js`.
