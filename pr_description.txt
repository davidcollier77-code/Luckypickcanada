🎨 Palette: Mobile Hero Alignment fix

**What:**
Removed `pt-4` in `HomePage.js` and `mt-2` in `Hero.js` to eliminate restrictive spacing and enable the main content and maple leaf logo to properly shift up to the top on mobile. Adjusted spacing on the navigation bar.

**Why:**
The previous padding and margin explicitly pushed the container down on mobile, preventing it from resting at the top viewport edge.

**Before/After:**
Before: The hero section had excessive spacing, restricting the UI vertically.
After: The hero block (content and logo) naturally moves upward to maximize vertical space, looking better on mobile screens.

**Accessibility:**
No accessibility changes; visually aligns with intended design specs.
