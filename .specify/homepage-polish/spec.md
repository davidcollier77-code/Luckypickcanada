# Spec

## Feature: Homepage Polish and SEO Refinement
This feature aims to refine the visuals of the homepage components (Lucky Stories and Suggestion Box) and update SEO metadata.

## User Stories
- **US1 (P1):** As a user, I want the Lucky Stories container to visually match the glass-panel appearance of the other cards.
- **US2 (P1):** As a user, I want the Suggestion Box security-check area to display correctly without broken images or large gray boxes before or after verification.
- **US3 (P1):** As a search engine, I want accurate title, description, and structured data representing the entertainment-only nature of the site.
- **US4 (P1):** As a visually impaired user, I want the hero image to have an accurate alternative text.
- **US5 (P1):** As a user, I want the closing About section to be readable against the Milky Way background and not overly repetitive.
