# Lucky Meter cinematic audio — sources and licences

Two new sound effects were sourced online on 2026-11-15 for the Lucky Meter
cinematic redesign. Both are real studio/library recordings downloaded from
Mixkit. Nothing here is synthesised, generated by AI, or produced with a sound
generator. The existing Lucky Meter cues (buildup, meteor whoosh, lightning
impact, firework burst, whistle and willow crackle) are unchanged.

## 1. Finale boom

- **Title:** Clear firework explosions
- **Provider / creator:** Mixkit, by Envato. The listing credits the Mixkit
  library rather than an individual recordist.
- **Sound page:** https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/fireworks/ (item
  “Clear firework explosions”)
- **Original audio file:** https://assets.mixkit.co/active_storage/sfx/2994/2994.wav

## Licence verification

The Mixkit Sound Effects Free License page states that items “can be used in
your commercial and non-commercial projects for free”, and that the licensee may
“download, copy, modify, distribute and publicly perform the Sound Effect Items
on any web or social media platform”. It permits use in an End Product that
incorporates the item and is larger in scope and different in nature than the
item. It does not require attribution and does not permit redistributing the
sound on its own as stock, in a tool or template, or with source files. This
repository uses both files only as integrated cues inside the Lucky Meter
experience, which is an end product; the source is recorded here regardless.

## Derivatives

Both derivatives are mono 16-bit PCM at 44.1 kHz with short fades so they start
and end cleanly. They were produced from the downloaded masters with Python’s
standard `wave`/`array` modules (no DSP library, no synthesis):

| Local file | Source | Duration | Peak | RMS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lucky-meter-finale-boom.wav` | 2994, full recording, 6 ms fade-in, 300 ms fade-out | 2.280 s | 0.904 | 0.088 |
| `lucky-meter-thunder-rumble.wav` | 1296, first 8.0 s, 50 ms fade-in, 1.3 s fade-out | 8.000 s | 0.954 | 0.163 |

`lucky-meter-finale-boom.wav` is played at 0.96 volume on the finale cue;
`lucky-meter-thunder-rumble.wav` is played at 0.22–0.32 with rate variation and
is throttled to at most two rumbles per lightning event. The existing lightning
impact stays at its original volume and file.

## Use in the product

`components/DailyResonance.tsx` loads both files with the existing Howler
pattern on the visitor’s Start gesture. The finale boom is cued at the golden
burst; thunder follows the first and final lightning strikes only.
