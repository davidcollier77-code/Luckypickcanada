## Toolkit render verification (software WebGL)

Because the toolkit has no production consumer yet, it was bundled with esbuild outside the app
(entry under `node_modules/.cache`, bundle in `/tmp`, nothing committed) and opened in the
repository Chromium through `./scripts/playwright-cli.sh`.

Scene: `MeshStandardMaterial` gold torus knot (metalness 1, roughness 0.22), `RoomEnvironment`
PMREM environment, directional + point + ambient lights, ACES tone mapping, bloom composer.

Observed result (`window.__gfxResult`):

```json
{"stageCreated":true,"effectsCreated":true,"frames":34,
 "pixels":{"width":640,"height":360,"lit":230400,"max":255,"mean":130},
 "error":null,"webglVersion":"WebGL2",
 "renderer":"ANGLE (Google, Vulkan 1.3.0 (SwiftShader Device (Subzero) (0x0000C0DE)), SwiftShader driver)",
 "glError":0,"consoleErrors":[],"bloom":true,"postScale":1}
```

- Stage and cinematic effects created; bloom active on the `high` tier.
- 34 frames rendered through the adaptive loop; `gl.getError() === 0`.
- No page error; the only console output was the browser's `favicon.ico` 404 on the harness page.
- Readback shows a rendered, lit frame; capture: `artifacts/graphics-development-toolkit/toolkit-render.png`.
- Renderer is SwiftShader (no hardware GPU in this VM), so this proves correctness, not GPU speed.
- A first-frame delta of ~0.13 s reflects shader compilation and PMREM setup, not steady-state cost.
