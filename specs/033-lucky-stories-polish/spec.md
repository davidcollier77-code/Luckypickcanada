# Lucky Stories Visual Polish Spec

## Task Group
Polishing

## Scope
- Refine general visual finish (crisp, cohesive, premium) for Lucky Stories/Map.
- Update primary gold buttons (3D finish, subtle gradients/shadows).
- Update secondary buttons (dark charcoal/near-black, gold accents).
- Dimensional polish for the four-leaf clover (subtle shading, soft highlights).
- Apply consistent translucent dark styling to story cards (remove current green-filled selection state).
- Ensure "View full story" smoothly expands and scrolls into view, avoiding header overlap.
- Preserve existing functionality, artwork, AVIF/WebP image optimizations, and content.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Primary buttons feel tactile with a raised gold appearance.
- Secondary buttons are distinct, using a dark theme with gold accents.
- The four-leaf clover has subtle depth without altering its proportions.
- Story cards use a translucent dark treatment, allowing the Milky Way background to show through.
- Expanding a story triggers a smooth scroll to make the content fully visible.
- No existing layout, functionality, or image optimizations are broken.
