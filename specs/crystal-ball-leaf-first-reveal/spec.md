# Crystal Ball Leaf-First Fortune Reveal

## User story
As a visitor asking the Crystal Ball a question, I want the orb's energy flash and a falling maple-leaf cue to lead into the answer so the result feels deliberate and readable.

## Acceptance criteria
- The orb's energy-gathering effect begins immediately after "Seek Fortune" is pressed.
- Once the answer is available, a short golden pre-reveal flash appears before any answer characters are shown.
- A visually consistent duplicate leaf animates from the existing Crystal Ball artwork into the fortune panel. The source artwork and decorative leaves remain unchanged.
- Character-by-character answer typing starts only after the leaf flight completes.
- The completion pulse remains after typing finishes.
- Reduced-motion preferences skip the decorative flight and show the answer without character-by-character animation.
- Visitors can ask at least two questions in the same visit; no arbitrary question-count cap is introduced.
- Existing fortune text remains available to screen readers and unrelated artwork/layout remains unchanged.
