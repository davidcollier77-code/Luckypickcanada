# Quickstart — Crystal Ball Leaf-First Reveal

1. Open /crystal-ball on mobile and desktop.
2. Enter a non-empty question and press Seek Fortune.
3. Confirm the loading energy glow begins immediately.
4. Confirm a distinct gold pre-reveal flash appears before any visible answer characters.
5. Confirm one duplicate golden leaf travels from the artwork area to the answer panel while original artwork remains unchanged.
6. Confirm the typewriter starts only after the leaf lands, then the completion pulse runs.
7. Submit a second question and confirm a second session reading is recorded.
8. Repeat with reduced motion enabled; answer text should appear without the decorative flight or typewriter.
