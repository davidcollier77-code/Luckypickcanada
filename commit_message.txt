🎨 Palette: [UX improvement] Enhance Lucky Meter Reveal Pacing

**What:**
Shortened the reveal pacing of the Lucky Meter to 4.5 seconds and implemented a cinematic percentage convergence effect.

**Why:**
To improve the user experience by making the reveal snappier and feeling less randomized ("slot-machine" like) during the build up. The numbers now elegantly lock into place.

**Before/After:**
- **Before:** Random numbers flashed wildly between 0-100 for 9 seconds.
- **After:** The sequence takes 4.5 seconds. A quadratic easing function quickly rolls the numbers and then decelerates towards the final score smoothly.

**Accessibility:**
The visual logic maintains its current boundaries and no excessive animations were added to reduce motion.
