ROOT CAUSE:
The imperceptible animation was caused by multiple factors in `app/homepage/HomePage.js`:
1. Twinkle speed was too slow (taking 3-6 seconds per cycle) and the amplitude combined with very dim base opacities made changes unnoticeable. Only 8% of stars could twinkle.
2. The shooting star timer delay was set to 30-60 seconds, meaning it rarely triggered within normal viewing times (e.g., a 44s recording).
3. The perceived softness was caused by sub-pixel `ctx.fillRect` sizes (radii as small as 0.2px) which the browser antialiases into blurry blocks.
4. CSS on `.homepage-star-canvas` had `opacity: 0.74;` and `filter: saturate(0.9) brightness(0.95);`, washing out the brightness of the generated stars.

TWINKLE CHANGES:
- Increased twinkling subset from 8% to 12%.
- Adjusted `twinkleSpeed` from a max of `0.003` to `0.006` (radians per ms) for gentle but perceptible movement.
- Increased sine wave amplitude from `0.3` to `0.4` for greater contrast.
- Increased minimum `baseAlpha` from `0.1` to `0.2` so twinkling stars don't entirely vanish for long periods.
- Redundant `globalCompositeOperation = 'screen'` overridden immediately by `'source-over'` was removed.

SHOOTING-STAR CHANGES:
- Adjusted schedule timeout from `Math.random() * 30000 + 30000` to `Math.random() * 20000 + 10000` (10s to 30s), ensuring it triggers occasionally during a normal visit.

STARFIELD SHARPNESS CHANGES:
- Changed `initAmbientStars` radii generation to `Math.random() * 0.8 + 0.5`. Enforcing a minimum radius of 0.5px (1x1 full pixel minimum) eliminates browser antialiasing blur on sub-pixels.
- Updated `themes/default/homepage.css` and `public/themes/default/homepage.css` to remove the `.homepage-star-canvas` filter, and increased opacity to 0.95 (desktop) and 0.85 (mobile).
